# The Ponderwump — Brand Guidelines

*Version 2.0 — the single source of truth for the book, the website, and every piece of marketing. Direction: **hand-lettered** (Oliver Jeffers-adjacent) type in **Shantell Sans**, with a **warm Deep Amber ochre** palette. If something you're making doesn't feel like it belongs next to the reference illustrations, it's off-brand.*

---

## 1. Brand essence

**One line:** *A mind is a garden no engine can grow.*

**What we are:** a warm, hand-painted, rhyming picture book for ages 2–3 about a small girl with a big mind, a dad who forgets how to wonder, and a gentle giant made real by belief. Underneath the story sit four quiet lessons in *thinking* (ordering, breaking down, repeating, planning).

**Feeling to evoke:** warm dusk-lit wonder, hand-made and personal. The whole brand should feel *drawn by hand* — like a story someone lovingly lettered themselves (the Oliver Jeffers tradition), not typeset by a machine. That hand-made feel is thematically on-point: this is a book about the human mind versus the machine.

**Brand personality:** gentle, clever, a little playful, hand-made, never slick. Speaks to the child first and winks at the grown-up second.

**We are NOT:** cartoonish-corporate, neon, flat-vector, edtech-y, or preachy. Never say "STEM." Never explain the joke. Never look machine-perfect.

## 2. Colour palette — Deep Amber (warm ochre)

Warm, glowing, dusk-lit — pulled from the lantern-lit reference art and warmed toward ochre.

### Core

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | **Deep Amber** | `#B4791E` | The signature warm ochre — headings, key panels, brand colour |
| Warm accent | **Lantern Gold** | `#E8B54D` | Glow, highlights, the giant's amber eyes, joy |
| Secondary warm | **Soft Clay** | `#C47B5A` | Terracotta rooftops, warm mid-tones, secondary surfaces |
| Accent (sparingly) | **Rust** | `#9C3D1C` | The single most important thing — CTAs, love, emphasis |
| Signature green | **Dark Olive** | `#556B2F` | The Ponderwump's coat, nature, grounding |
| Ink / text | **Near-Black Ink** | `#1F1811` | Body text, hand-lettering, night |

### Neutrals & paper

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page / paper | **Warm Paper** | `#F4EAD3` | Text-page backgrounds, site light sections |
| Antique cream | **Antique Cream** | `#EAD9B5` | Cards, warm surfaces, reversed text on ink |
| Muted line | **Pencil Brown** | `#6E5A42` | Captions, borders, secondary text |

### Usage rules
- **60 / 30 / 10.** ~60% warm paper/cream + ink, ~30% Deep Amber + Soft Clay, ~10% Rust/Lantern Gold accents. Rust is a spice, never a base.
- Lead everything warm — this palette has **no cool base**. Night scenes go warm-dark (Near-Black Ink warmed brown), never blue-grey.
- Rust is reserved for the single most important thing on any surface (one CTA, one accent).
- Lantern Gold = glow/magic/warmth. Great for small highlights and the amber eyes; not a large-area UI colour.
- Dark Olive grounds compositions and ties to the Ponderwump; use for nature and as a calm secondary.

## 3. Typography — hand-lettered

**Shantell Sans** for *everything* — a genuine hand-drawn variable font, playful and warm, in the Oliver Jeffers hand-lettered tradition. One family, used at different weights, keeps the whole brand feeling personally drawn and cohesive.

| Role | Setting | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Book title / display | Shantell Sans **Bold / Extra Bold** | The hand-lettered "cover" feel. |
| Book body (read-aloud) | Shantell Sans **Regular**, 22–28pt | Generous, storybook, warm. |
| Site headings | Shantell Sans Bold | |
| Site body / UI | Shantell Sans Regular/Medium | Highly legible at its informal weights. |
| Captions | Shantell Sans Medium, tracked | |

Shantell Sans is free/OFL — safe for print embedding and web. It has an *"Informal"* and a *"Normal"* style axis; use **Informal** for the hand-drawn character, **Normal** where you need slightly steadier reading (long paragraphs on the site).

**Fallback stack:** `'Shantell Sans', 'Comic Sans MS', cursive` (Comic Sans is the least-bad ubiquitous hand fallback; the web font will almost always load first).

### Type rules
- Book title always: **The Ponderwump** in Shantell Sans, never all-caps-tracked-out, never a different family.
- One family everywhere. The *hand* is the brand — don't dilute it with a second typeface.
- Book verse: never break a rhyming couplet across a page turn.
- Keep line-length on site to 60–75 characters; a hand font tires the eye faster at long measures.

## 4. Voice & tone

**Voice (constant):** warm, rhythmic, curious, gentle. British spelling (Mummy, Daddy, colour, marvellous).

**Tone (flexes):**
- *Book:* lyrical, rhyming, read-aloud. Trust the child.
- *Site / store:* warm and plain-spoken to parents. Sell the feeling ("a bedtime story that quietly teaches a child to think"), not the mechanics.
- *Social:* playful, one wink per post (strawberry, "the cloud", the "shipped it, skipped it" squirrel, the rubber duck).

### Do / Don't
- ✅ "A rhyming bedtime adventure about a brave little thinker."
- ✅ "Big feelings, a gentle giant, and a mind that's all your own."
- ❌ "Teaches computational thinking & STEM fundamentals!"
- ❌ Exclamation-mark spam. Baby-talk to parents. Lecturing anyone.

**Tagline (primary):** *A mind is a garden no engine can grow.*
Alternates: *Believe in the things you cannot yet see.* / *For brave little thinkers.*

## 5. Illustration & imagery

The reference style is the law; the palette is now warm-ochre.

- **Medium look:** watercolour + coloured-pencil + gouache. Visible paper grain, loose ink linework, painterly washes. NOT digital-flat, NOT 3D, NOT vector.
- **Light:** low, warm, dusk/night — but **warm-dark** (amber, ochre, rust, warm shadow), not cool teal. Glowing lantern light.
- **Composition:** atmospheric depth, tall trees, tumbling rooftops, tiny figures in big worlds.
- **Palette:** the Deep Amber palette above.
- **Faces:** simple, soft, expressive — small dot/line features.
- **Never:** stock photos of people in marketing (except author photo). Any lifestyle imagery must be illustrated.

---

## 6. Layout & spacing

**Spacing scale (4pt base):** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 px.

- Generous whitespace (paper) around text. Storybooks breathe.
- Site: max content width ~1120px; 24–32px gutters; section padding 64–96px.
- Rounded corners: soft, small (8–12px).
- Shadows: soft, warm-tinted, never hard grey.
- A little hand-drawn irregularity is welcome (slightly rotated stamps, uneven rules) — reinforces the made-by-hand feel.

---

## 7. Logo / wordmark

- Primary wordmark: **"The Ponderwump"** hand-lettered in Shantell Sans Bold, Near-Black Ink on Warm Paper (or Antique Cream reversed on Ink/Amber).
- Optional mark: the giant's two **curling question-mark horns** — a simple painted glyph for favicon / avatar.
- Clear space: at least the cap-height of "P" all sides.
- Don't: stretch, recolour outside palette, add hard drop shadows, set in a different font.

## 8. Applications quick-reference

- **Book cover:** warm dusk Muddlewood, the giant revealed with glowing amber eyes, hand-lettered title, ochre/rust warmth.
- **Website hero:** warm-dark illustration, hand-lettered wordmark + tagline, one Rust "Buy on Amazon" button.
- **Social avatar:** horns glyph on Deep Amber.
- **Amazon A+ / thumbnails:** keep the hand-lettered wordmark legible small; lead with the giant + amber eyes.
